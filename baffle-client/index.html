How to play

01

Trace a word. Drag across neighboring letters—up, down, sideways, or diagonal—then release to score.

02

Mind your paws. You cannot use the same tile twice in one word.

03

Score big. 3–4 letters score 1, then longer words climb fast.

04

Watch the pulse. The live panel shows who leads and who has found words recently.

05

Race together. Everyone hunts the same grid. First to find it does not matter—your score does.
