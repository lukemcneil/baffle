The tiny rulebook

How to play

01

Trace a word. Drag loosely across neighboring letters—up, down, sideways, or diagonal. The grid snaps to your path.

02

Mind your paws. You cannot use the same tile twice in one word. Multi-letter tiles such as Qu supply all their letters at once.

03

Score big. Classic uses traditional Boggle values. Netflix-style gives one point for three letters, then one more per letter, with double points for a word only you found.

04

Watch the pulse. The live panel shows who leads and who has found words recently.

05

Know the room rule. If shared-word cancellation is on, any word found by multiple players scores zero for all of them.
